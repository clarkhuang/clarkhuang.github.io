# Disclaimer

*Silicon Valley C Players* is an **embellished fictional dramatization** that may or may not be based on true stories.

## Front matter (print, ebook, and every Markdown/HTML edition)

> **A note before we begin**
>
> This book is an embellished fictional dramatization. Some of it may be based on true stories. Some of it definitely isn't. We're not telling you which is which — that's half the fun.
>
> The narrator, Charlie Hack, is a fictional character loosely inspired by the author. Every other name, character, company, product, school, and venue in the story chapters is either the product of the author's imagination or used fictitiously. Many characters are composites, and events, dates, places, and details have been changed, combined, compressed, or invented for the sake of the story (and the joke). Any resemblance to actual persons, living or dead, or to actual companies or events, is coincidental and not intended. Nothing in the story chapters is a statement of fact about any real person or company.
>
> The real-world parts are labeled as such. The Foreword and **Part Five: How to Work Now** are nonfiction: the author's own opinions about software and AI, with real, cited public sources. Opinions are opinions; sources are linked so you can check them.
>
> This is an OpenBook. If you think you recognize yourself and you'd rather not, or something is wrong, tell us and we'll fix it: see [TAKEDOWN.md](TAKEDOWN.md).

## Notes for writers and AI agents

- The disclaimer is hygiene, not armor. It does **not** make it OK to portray a recognizable real person. Follow [book-source/NAMING-POLICY.md](book-source/NAMING-POLICY.md).
- Don't market the book with "these are my real stories" or "guess who this really is." Keep the "could that really have happened?" game about *events*, never about *who*.
