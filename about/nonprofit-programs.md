# Nonprofit programs and member project allowances

Program research checked November 26, 2026. These are published offers the proposed foundation could apply for if eligible. Approval, the award amount and permitted uses must be confirmed with each provider.

| Provider | Possible use and eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| GitHub | Registered nonprofits can apply for a free Team plan for the foundation’s repositories, code review and deployment controls. [GitHub for Nonprofits](https://docs.github.com/en/nonprofit/nonprofit-teams-plan/getting-started-with-the-github-team-plan-for-nonprofits) |
| Anthropic | Eligible nonprofits can receive up to 75% off Claude Team and Enterprise plans for people building and teaching the labs. Budget separately for model API calls from running apps unless a grant explicitly covers them. [Claude for Nonprofits](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-nonprofits) |
| Twilio | Impact Access offers eligible organizations free units and discounts on selected products for mission-related communications. Confirm which voice, messaging or email costs qualify and whether a member project is covered. [Impact Access](https://www.twilio.org/en-us/support-and-resources/impact-access-program) |
| Google | Eligible organizations can activate a no-cost Workspace edition. In the US, Google requires qualifying 501(c)(3) status or a verified group exemption. Fiscal sponsorship alone does not establish eligibility. [Workspace offer](https://support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/18865828) · [Eligibility](https://support.google.com/nonprofits/answer/3215869?hl=en) |
| Stripe | Eligible nonprofits can request reduced processing fees when at least 80% of their Stripe payment volume comes from tax-deductible donations. Budget independently for member sales and onboard each seller through the platform’s payments provider. [Nonprofit pricing](https://support.stripe.com/questions/fee-discount-for-nonprofit-organizations) · [Connect onboarding](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/embedded-onboarding) |

## Funding a member app

A builder would sign in with GitHub and see the allowance, what it covers and what happens at the limit. Grants could combine donor funds, inference on foundation-owned hardware and credits that specifically cover the project. Estimate costs at ordinary rates until the provider confirms coverage. Record which projects may use each award, its expiry, and who covers costs after it runs out.

Render’s acceptable-use policy restricts providing its service to third parties. Before offering independent member deployments, obtain written confirmation for sponsored prototypes, commercial apps and cost recovery, or select a host whose agreement covers those uses. The team can develop the course with foundation-owned sample apps in the meantime. Budget for MongoDB Atlas at ordinary rates until applicable credits are confirmed.
