Sidebar: Nothing new on race day. I have a story for you. When foundations are stable, BFCM is just another Thursday. This is not AI, it's me.

There is a golden rule in training versus competition: nothing new on race day. BFCM is the company's race day. If you're prepared, then it's a Thursday. Don't listen to all the noise.

Don't run around with the chickens. Be the pigs when the going gets tough. You've all heard the eggs-and-bacon story in Scrum. A chicken and a pig want to open a restaurant. "What should we call it?" says the pig. The chicken says, "How about Ham & Eggs?" The pig says, "Uh no thanks. You'd be involved, but I'd be committed."

Hold others accountable. That's my only advice. The running version of this rule — twenty weeks of training for one Boston-qualifier shot, long runs that top out at 20 or 21 miles, not 26, and race day as just another Saturday — is in Chapter 17b, Building Blocks.		outlined · has Clark's story · needs a story
