Open the Source

1:44·Narrated in Clark's AI voice·Download MP4Not watched✓Watched

Up next: Lesson 14: Ask It to Disagree

The lesson

AI-supplied citations can be perfectly formatted and completely invented, and pushed on a dead link a model may insist the page changed. A reference you haven't opened is a claim, not a source.

The rule · for your agents

Open it before you use it: click the link, check the title and authors, find the sentence you need on the page. Nothing to open? Not a source yet.
