AI Tips · Lesson 1

Paste First, Ask Second

1:06·Narrated in Clark's AI voice·Download MP4Not watched✓Watched

Up next: Lesson 2: Ask for Proof, Not “Done”

The lesson

When an AI gives you a useless answer, it is usually not the AI's fault: you described your problem instead of showing it. Without the real material, it can only guess. With a screenshot, the document, or the full error message in front of it, the same question gets a specific, usable answer.

The rule · for your agents

Paste first, ask second. Hand over the screenshot, document, error message, or email thread before you type a single word of your question.
