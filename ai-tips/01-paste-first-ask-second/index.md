## The lesson

When an AI gives you a useless answer, it is usually not the AI's fault: you described the problem instead of showing it. Without the real material, it can only guess. Paste the screenshot, document, error message, or email thread first, and the same question gets a specific, usable answer.

## The rule

Paste first, ask second. Hand over the screenshot, document, error message, or email thread before you type a single word of your question.

This rule is written so an AI agent can follow it as a standing instruction.

## Transcript

When an A.I. gives you a useless answer, it's usually not the A.I.'s fault. You described your problem, instead of showing it.

Here's how it usually goes. You open a chat, and type: why is my report wrong?

But it's never seen your report. So it guesses. Five generic possibilities, and not one of them is yours.

Now flip it. Screenshot the report, and paste it in first. Then ask the exact same question.

Same question. A completely different answer. Because now it can actually see what you're asking about.

Look at what it had to work with. Five words, versus the whole report. That gap is the difference between an answer you argue with, and one you can use.

And it's not just reports. An error? Paste the full message. An email reply? The whole thread. A formula that's off? The sheet. If it would have to guess, hand it over.

So here's the rule. Paste first, ask second. Whatever you're asking about, hand it over before you type a single word.

It takes four seconds. And it saves the three rounds of back-and-forth you were about to have.
