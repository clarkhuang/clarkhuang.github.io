Your Bot in the Meeting

2:17·Narrated in Clark's AI voice·Download MP4Not watched✓Watched

Up next: Lesson 12: Brief the Next Chat

The lesson

A notetaker records one call and summarises it. A bot that's connected to your team's work can answer questions live, turn action items into real tickets, and remember what was decided last week. This tip shows how to try it on one meeting, and the limit that catches people out.

The rule · for your agents

Try a connected team bot on one recurring meeting, the one where someone always says "I'll check and get back to you." Invite it (or give it the link) and admit it from the waiting room. Afterwards, if you invited it, share the notes yourself, because often only the inviter receives them. Expect a first version: it may not see screen shares or speak aloud yet.
