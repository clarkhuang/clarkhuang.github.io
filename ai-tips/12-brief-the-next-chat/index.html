The lesson

Long chats drift without warning: early decisions count for less and can fall out of view. When a chat gets long, have it write a handoff and carry on in a fresh chat.

The rule · for your agents

When a chat gets long, brief the next one: the goal and the limits, every decision so far, what's still open, and read it before you paste it.
