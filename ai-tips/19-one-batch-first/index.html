AI Tips / Lesson 19

One Batch First

Practice a short Slack conversation with an AI assistant. Review one reminder together before asking for the rest.

The rule

Ask the AI for one example and tell it to stop. Check the example, give one correction if needed, then tell it when to continue.

Read the transcript

Imagine you're organizing a picnic. Your team left you a list of reminders to tidy up. The first says, setup at nine, bring folding chairs. You want the reminders to sound friendly.

In your team chat, ask the AI to rewrite the first reminder only. Say, make it friendly, keep the details, and stop there so I can read it. Now you can see what it thinks friendly means.

Here's a possible reply: Come help us set up at nine! Sounds friendly. But imagine you're the volunteer walking out the door. What were you supposed to take with you? The folding chairs.

Reply in the same chat: keep the chairs, and try that one again. Now it says, Join us at nine for setup. Please bring folding chairs! Read it back. Time, task, and chairs are all there.

Now tell the AI to use that style for the rest and keep what to bring in every reminder. Give the finished list a read before sending it. One quick check up front saved you from fixing the same mistake in every line.

Next time a job comes with a whole list, try saying these words: show me the first one, then wait. You can bring that habit to whatever you ask AI to help with next. See you in the next tip.
