The Label Is Not the Definition

1:55·Narrated in Clark's AI voice·Download MP4Not watched✓Watched

Up next: Lesson 11: Your Bot in the Meeting

The lesson

The most common mistake in six weeks of bots' self-written lessons: trusting what a field is called instead of what it counts. A number with the wrong definition still ties out and reconciles, so nothing looks wrong and it travels.

The rule · for your agents

Before using or explaining a number, find out what it actually counts. Read the definition, not the field name. Treat a surprising number as a likely definition problem before treating it as a story. When the source is unclear, ask the data owner which table holds the number they actually sign off on, and say which dataset every reported number came from.
