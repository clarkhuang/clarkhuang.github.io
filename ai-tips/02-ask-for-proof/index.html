AI Tips · Lesson 2

Ask for Proof, Not “Done”

2:31·Narrated in Clark's AI voiceNot watched✓Watched

The lesson

When a bot says “done”, it is usually reporting the status of the step it ran, not whether the outcome you wanted is true. Five short examples show the gap. None of them is a bot misbehaving: each reported honestly on the step it ran. The fix is to ask for something only a real result can produce.

The rule · for your agents

Ask for the artifact, not the status: the ticket numbers, a screenshot on the actual device, counts per item instead of a job status, a link you can click yourself. No artifact means it hasn't happened yet.
