The lesson

Agents act on real systems, and a rule typed into a prompt is not a lock. Put the lock in access and approval settings, and keep the judgment for steps you can't undo.

The rule · for your agents

Set the stop before you start: say what done looks like, limit what it can touch, require approval before delete, reset, push or send, and watch the first few steps.
