AI Tips · Lesson 15

Treat Memory Like Notes

1:38·Narrated in Clark's AI voice·Download MP4Not watched✓Watched

Up next: Lesson 16: Don't Fire and Forget

The lesson

AI memory is a small set of saved notes, not a mind. Notes can be never saved, saved wrong, off-topic or stale, and they won't keep up unless you update them.

The rule · for your agents

Treat memory like notes: say what matters in the prompt, don't assume a new chat knows, read your memory now and then, and fix or delete what's wrong.
