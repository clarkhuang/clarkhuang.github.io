# Silicon Valley C Players — outline

Working public outline of Clark Huang's OpenBook, an **embellished fictional dramatization** that may or may not be based on true stories. In the story chapters the narrator (Charlie Hack), people, companies, schools and places are fictional or used fictitiously; see [NAMING-POLICY.md](NAMING-POLICY.md) and [../DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md). Status marks what is already on the record (**HAVE**), what has a start but still needs interviews or writing (**PARTIAL**), and what is only a seed or a slot (**NEED**). Nothing here invents a story. Hooks use only what Clark has already published or approved as story seeds.

The published preview is at [https://clarkhuang.github.io/book/](https://clarkhuang.github.io/book/).

**Throughline:** A C player who kept going, dreamed in green screen, watched AI come for the coders first, and now shows you how to work.
