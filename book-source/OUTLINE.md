# Silicon Valley C Players — story arc and chapters

Working public outline of Clark Huang's OpenBook, an **embellished fictional dramatization** that may or may not be based on true stories. In the story chapters the narrator (Charlie Hack), people, companies, schools and places are fictional or used fictitiously; see [NAMING-POLICY.md](NAMING-POLICY.md) and [../DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md). The how-to half is nonfiction: Clark's views, with sources.

The published preview is at [https://clarkhuang.github.io/book/](https://clarkhuang.github.io/book/). The public page for this outline is [https://clarkhuang.github.io/book/story-arc/](https://clarkhuang.github.io/book/story-arc/).

**This outline will change** as chapters are written, as interviews fill gaps, and as people contribute.

**Throughline:** A C player who kept going, dreamed in green screen, watched AI come for the coders first, and now shows you how to work. In the nonfiction half, Clark puts his advice to work on the book and site themselves. Readers can inspect the result, learn from the misses, and use what works to build something of their own.

The book has two halves. The first follows the career arc: school days, then the jobs in career order, then the turn when AI came for the code writers. The second half is about this site — how to work now, the OpenBook itself, how agents can use and improve the site — and where it goes next.

Status marks what is already on the record (**drafted**), what has a start but is not yet a finished chapter (**outlined**), and what is only a seed or a slot (**planned**). A chapter marked **needs a story** has no source story yet. Nothing here invents a story. Hooks use only what Clark has already published or approved as story seeds.
