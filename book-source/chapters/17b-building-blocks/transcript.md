## Progressive Overload

1:42 · `07_milo` · 17.85s

You master the bodyweight positions first, then you add load. Progressive overload is how you build strength over time: a little more, then a little more. The legend is of Milo of Croton, a Greek wrestler in the sixth century B.C., who carried a newborn calf every day until it was a grown bull.

2:00 · `08_overload` · 13.89s

You master the bodyweight positions first, then you add load, a little more, then a little more. Then you put that strength into the lifts. Fundamentals, then building blocks, then intensity, or more strength. It's all the same pattern.

## The Order

2:14 · `09_mci` · 18.45s

The order I kept coming back to is mechanics first, then consistency, and only then intensity. I was after virtuosity: years of practice, not a shortcut. That sequence is the method I actually used: not a complete program written in advance, but the next block, then the next one.

## The Order
