# Guest stories

This folder is for short first-person guest pieces published under the contributor’s own name, as themselves — not as a fictional character in the book.

That is different from a regular “share a story” submission. Those may be renamed and fictionalized, and you are not named as a character. A guest story keeps your name on the piece, if Clark accepts it.

## How to submit

1. You may file an issue first if you want to talk the idea through. To submit the story itself, open a pull request against the `next-revision` branch.
2. Add one new Markdown file in this folder. Name it like `your-handle-short-title.md`.
3. Use the format below.
4. The file must include your own written consent to publish your name.
5. Do not include other people’s real names, private details, confidential or NDA material, customer data, or security details.
6. Clark reviews the pull request. Nothing is published until he merges it. He may edit, ask for changes, or decline the piece.

Guest stories are licensed like other book text: [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](../LICENSE), plus the contributor terms in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Clark’s editorial discretion still applies.

## Format

```markdown
# Title

**By:** Your name (the name you consent to publish)
**GitHub:** @your-handle
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Consent

I, [your name], consent to this first-person story being published on
clarkhuang.github.io and in *Silicon Valley C Players*, under my name as
written above. I have the right to share this account. It contains no
confidential, NDA, customer, security, or deal information, and no real
names of other private people.

## The story

[Your first-person account, in your own words.]
```

Accepted guest stories are published at [/book/guest/](https://clarkhuang.github.io/book/guest/) and credited on the [Contributors](https://clarkhuang.github.io/contributors/) page.
