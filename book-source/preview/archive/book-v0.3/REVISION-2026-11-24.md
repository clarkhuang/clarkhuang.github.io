# Preview revision — 24 November 2026

A ghostwritten pass on the mid-book preview, then a re-record, so the narration flows more smoothly, says the letter A in “A players” correctly, and matches the own-workflow wording of the AI-and-code claim.

Claude Fable rewrote the script from the current mid-book preview and from Clark's own confirmed lines. Meanings and facts were kept; no new stories, claims, numbers, or names were added. After Fable returned, one line in `12_how_to` was tightened: “have to change around that” became “have to be geared around that,” so the original verb stayed.

The previous mid-book files are archived in [archive/book-v0.2](archive/book-v0.2/).

## Pronunciation

Speech-to-text on the previous recording found the letter A read as the article “uh” in two places:

- `04_myth`, “a story about A players,” around 0:29. This is the passage Clark flagged. Confidence on the letter A was only 0.53.
- `03_middle`, “C Things That Looked Like A Things,” around 0:26, heard as “a-things.”

“an A thing” in `05_outside` was probably already clear, because of “an,” but the performance text still uses “an Ay thing” to be safe. “A C player” (the article plus the letter C) and “Silicon Valley C Players” were already heard correctly and were left alone in the TTS text.

Phonetic spellings such as “Ay players” appear only in the performance / TTS text (`tts` in `script.json`). Captions and transcripts keep “A players.”
