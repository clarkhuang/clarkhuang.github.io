# Corrections and takedown requests

This is an OpenBook. If something in the book, the audiobook, the videobook, the site, or an issue or pull request in this repo affects you, we want to hear about it.

## When to use this

- You think a character or scene identifies you (or someone you represent), and you'd like it changed or removed.
- Something private or confidential about you, your family, or your company appears anywhere in this repo.
- A contributor's issue, comment, or pull request names you or says something about you.
- Something infringes your copyright (see "Copyright" below).

For ordinary story corrections or your side of a story, use the [Correction](https://github.com/clarkhuang/clarkhuang.github.io/issues/new?template=correction.yml) template instead.

## How to ask

1. **Public route:** open a [Takedown request](https://github.com/clarkhuang/clarkhuang.github.io/issues/new?template=takedown-request.yml). It gets the `takedown-request` label. **Don't repeat the sensitive detail in the issue** — just point to the chapter, page, issue, or timestamp.
2. **Private route:** if you'd rather not post publicly, email [clark75-7bw9ct@mail.grokbot.com](mailto:clark75-7bw9ct@mail.grokbot.com) with the subject "Takedown request."

## What happens next

- **Acknowledge within 7 days.** Clark (or his assistant) replies on the issue or privately.
- **Interim action.** If the request is credible, the passage is hidden or replaced on the `next-revision` branch while we look at it, and on `main` if it's already published. For an issue or comment in this repo, we hide or edit it and delete the old revision from the edit history.
- **Decision.** We'll usually rename, change identifying details, rewrite, or remove the passage. We don't need you to prove anything to ask. Clark makes the final editorial call.
- **Release.** Fixes land in the next tagged edition. Previous editions remain archived as GitHub Releases; if a legal or privacy issue affects an archived edition, we'll replace or withdraw that release asset where we can.
- **Log.** We keep a short, non-identifying log in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) ("Ch 10: identifying details changed after a request").
