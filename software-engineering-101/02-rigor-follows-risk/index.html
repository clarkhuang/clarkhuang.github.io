Software Engineering 101 · Lesson 2

Rigor Follows Risk

4:15·Narrated in Clark's AI voiceNot watched✓Watched

The lesson

A patch in ten minutes or the root cause in two days? Redeploy speed is only half the question: a redeploy fixes the code, but it doesn't undo what the code did. Sort work into two kinds. Product shape is uncertain and easy to change, so ship it rough behind a flag. Correctness you can't undo means money, customer data, and messages, so find the root cause.

The rule · for your agents

Rough is fine for product shape. It is never fine for money, customer data, or anything that can't be undone. If a piece of work is both, the stricter standard wins. For that kind of work: stop any live damage first, then find the root cause, and give the estimate in usage, not in days.
