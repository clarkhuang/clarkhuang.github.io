Software Engineering 101 · Lesson 1

Plan, Build, Verify (and When to Skip It)

4:41·Narrated in Clark's AI voiceNot watched✓Watched

The lesson

How do you fix code you don't understand when you only get one shot? Split the work into roles: a person who owns the problem, a planner, a builder, and a verifier on a different model that can only read and report. Then loop until the verifier runs out of serious things to say. The lesson also covers what that costs, and when a lighter path is good enough.

The rule · for your agents

If a change can't be rolled back, and the person asking can't check it themselves, it gets an independent review, on a different model, before it ships. That's the floor. The longer list, of when to use the full pattern, is judgment.
