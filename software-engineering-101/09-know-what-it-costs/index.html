Software Engineering 101 · Lesson 9

Know What It Costs

2:38·Narrated in Clark's AI voice·Download MP4Not watched✓Watched

Up next: Lesson 10: Strict About Data, Relaxed About the Perimeter

The lesson

AI usage is the new currency of software. See the spend, the limit and a priced forecast before you start, and remember that owning software costs more than building it.

The rule · for your agents

Before any sizable task, show the spend so far, the limit, and a forecast for each path. If you can't see a number, say so, and label an estimate as an estimate. Afterward, record what it actually cost. And before you build something new, check what already exists.
