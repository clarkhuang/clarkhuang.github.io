About

Clark Huang

Clark Huang is a software engineer and technology executive with more than 30 years in the field. He studied computer science, with a minor in music, at the University of California, Berkeley from 1993 to 1997. He is Chief Technology Officer at Truly Free and Truly Free Home, a role he has held since 2025, and Co-Founder and CTO of eCIO, a nonprofit investment advisor, and its sister company eVestech, where he oversees technology development and technical operations. A seasoned technologist and serial founder, he is a believer in agile methods, pragmatism, continuous integration and delivery, test-driven development, and pair programming.
