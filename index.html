Software Engineering 301: Run Your Own AI Studio

The ten-lab playbook is available now. Videos and R&D field reports are coming, starting with a February 2027 build that puts a laptop and a 512 GB Mac Studio through the same real work.

Already running models on your own hardware? Tell us what you tried, what broke, and what you would do differently. Share your field notes.

Anyone curious about building software can start with one task they know well. Use SE 101 to make a small change and verify it, then use SE 201 to see what happens when several parts have to work together. Start with something you know firsthand: a volunteer shift your nonprofit struggles to fill, a booking you track by hand at your shop, or a tool you want to build for yourself.
