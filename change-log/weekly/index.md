Week of November 23, 2026

## The curriculum moved from local practice toward a member-platform design

SE 201 finished with an end-to-end capstone, SE 301 published a local-compute playbook and first runnable lesson, and SE 401 mapped a hosted-model build plan plus a proposed GitHub-only member platform. About now records possible nonprofit benefits and the provider limits that still need answers. The OpenBook offers two released chapter previews, while the site’s account and release tools remain the working examples learners can inspect today.

Week of November 16, 2026

## A library people and their agents could use

The opening weekend established the learning experience. A visitor could finish SE 101, listen to a book preview and bring an assistant to read the same material. The contribution and copy paths opened the work to other builders. Two recordings showed learners how to break a system into parts and integrate work from parallel agents.
