Week of November 23, 2026

The site became a worked example of the courses

The enterprise course finished with an end-to-end capstone, and SE 301 extended that practice to learners’ own machines. The site now exposes its own release record, so a learner can follow a change from a plain-language explanation to its source. About places the proposed nonprofit cohort alongside the material people can already use. If the learning service is waking up, GitHub sign-in now lets you wait on your article and retry there if needed.

Week of November 16, 2026

A library people and their agents could use

The opening weekend established the learning experience. A visitor could finish SE 101, listen to a book preview and bring an assistant to read the same material. The contribution and copy paths opened the work to other builders. Two recordings showed learners how to break a system into parts and integrate work from parallel agents.
