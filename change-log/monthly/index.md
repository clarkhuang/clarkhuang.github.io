## Nineteen AI tips and the first SE 301 and SE 401 videos

November produced complete introductory and enterprise courses, nineteen AI Tips, two released OpenBook chapter previews, and a local-compute course whose first lesson can be run now. The contribution, account and release surfaces make the site itself a worked example. SE 401 and About describe a proposed sponsored member platform, while explicitly leaving shared hosting, provider adapters, funding awards and local inference as work to validate. AI Tips 18 and 19 show how to catch a missing detail and review one example before expanding a job. SE 301 Video 1 tests a local model endpoint. SE 401 Video 1 uses a shift handoff to show how to check a model’s work. The release notes now link the four-video production release to the daily, weekly, monthly and archive entries.
