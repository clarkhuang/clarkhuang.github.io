## 2026-11 · An open engineering school takes shape

November brought together practical courses, the OpenBook and a contribution path for outside experience. Readers can watch a lesson, study its source or take a copy of the project. The new production archive gives them a way to check how that project evolves. About connects this work to the proposed foundation and names what a first cohort would still have to demonstrate.
