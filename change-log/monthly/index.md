## 2026-11 · An open engineering school takes shape

November established two connected ways to learn. The courses teach and demonstrate engineering practice, while the OpenBook tells the stories behind it and invites outside experience. A reader can use the public material, copy the project, and now save progress to an account. The local-compute playbook and proposed community cohort describe where that foundation could lead.
