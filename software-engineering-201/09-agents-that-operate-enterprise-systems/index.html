Agents That Operate Enterprise Systems Coming soon

Video coming soonThis lesson is in planning. The video will appear here when it's ready.

The idea

Agents that run the system, not just build it, with identity, least access, and an audit trail. The last step on the path: agents that operate enterprise systems day to day. Give each agent a clear job, its own identity, and the least access it needs, so every action is permitted, logged, and auditable. Picture a payments or investment system where an agent reconciles accounts overnight and flags anything that doesn't add up, while a person approves anything that moves money. Keep secrets and customer data out of reach, keep people at the decision points, and remember that you still own the outcome. Lesson 10, the capstone, puts all nine lessons to work on one small feature.

The premise of 201

101 was about one piece of work: one fix or one feature, done right with an AI loop of plan, build, and verify. 201 is about whole systems: how you design one, how the parts fit together, and how that has changed from thirty, twenty and ten years ago to now, with agents doing much of the building. It starts with the four eras of building systems, and what never changed. Then it builds the whole system AI-native, so agents and people can both read it, change it, and rebuild it. Then it climbs: decompose the system into a graph, run agents in parallel and bring the work back together, put people where the decisions matter, design quality in and watch every run, turn repeated work into skills, and wire those skills into routines and pipelines. Then come agents that operate enterprise systems, with real permissions, security, and an audit trail. It ends with a capstone that builds one small feature end to end, using every lesson in order. That is the move from one loop to enterprise systems.
