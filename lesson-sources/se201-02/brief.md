# Brief for Software Engineering 201, Lesson 1

Series: Software Engineering 201: From Loop to Enterprise Systems. 101 is one loop done right (plan, build, verify). 201 goes from that loop to enterprise systems: whole apps and systems built AI-native, ending with agents that operate enterprise systems with governance, permissions, and an audit trail.

Lesson 1 topic (Clark's direction): AI-native building of larger systems and apps.

Write a public, generic lesson of about four minutes in the house style of the Software Engineering 101 and 201 videos: a title card as the first frame, a quick question with three options and a reveal, a definition, one honest worked example, one plain analogy, the path for the rest of the series, one rule "for your agents", a reply ask about the viewer's own work, and "See you in lesson two."

Cover:
- What changes when you build a whole app or system with AI rather than one fix.
- AI-native means agents and people can both read, navigate, contribute to, and rebuild the system.
- Four things: Markdown twins, an index (llms.txt) and AGENTS.md files; specs and briefs as the source; tests and checks as the contract; staging plus a person's approval before production.
- Why this matters more at enterprise scale.
- Worked example: this site. Only claims that are true of the repository.

Constraints: no company-internal systems or details; no invented anecdotes in Clark's voice; no other people's names; no family or private information; no laugh or chuckle tags; engaging and concrete like a good Berkeley professor, never jokey.
